About This Portal

This portal is a demonstration project. Forms do not send real emails. In a live version, submissions would be reviewed by our team within 2–3 business days.

Expired opportunities are reviewed and removed on the first Monday of each month.

Scam Warning

If you have encountered a fraudulent opportunity, report it immediately using the "Report an Issue" form. You can also report employment scams to:

  • South African Police Service (SAPS): 10111
  • CIPC: cipc.co.za
  • Department of Labour: labour.gov.za
